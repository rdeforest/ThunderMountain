# Bend - River Town on the Chionthar
**Region**: Western Heartlands | **Founded**: 100+ years ago | **Era**: Before the Absolute Crisis (1492 DR) | **Status**: Draft

Items marked **[S]** are suggestions, not yet decided by Robert.

## Where It Is
- On the River Chionthar between **Berdusk** and **Iriaebor**, two cities that trade with each other and send barges up and down the river past Bend. **[S]**
- Built inside a large bend in the river, so it has water on three sides. The fourth side is the narrow neck of land where the road comes in.
- The river carries barges all the way up to Iriaebor. Just above Berdusk are the **Breaking Steps**, rapids that boats have to be winched through. That's a source of cargo delays and logistics jobs. **[S]**
- The middle Chionthar was quiet farm country in the 1300s. The one big exception everyone nationwide lived through was the Time of Troubles (1358 DR), when the gods walked the land. That would be a legend told by grandparents in Bend. **[S]**

## Character of the Town
- Diverse: all the common peoples live here, and nearly every language has more than one fluent speaker in town.
- Safer than the surrounding lands. Suzy is part of the reason.
- You can get any legal item, and a good many illegal ones, without much trouble. It's where goods change between road and river.
- A few logistics merchants have more work than they can handle.
- Several places of worship, a few pubs and inns.

## Economy
| Exports | Imports |
|---|---|
| Produce, lumber, furs, leather | Books, rare items, magic, advanced goods |

Shipping is the main business, with farming and lumber next.

## What's Wrong: The River Is Moving
Bend sits inside a river bend that is slowly turning into an oxbow. Some people say the river will move far enough that the docks will have to be rebuilt. Others call that fear-mongering, since everyone knows rivers don't move. Nobody does anything about it, and people talk about it the way they talk about sports or the weather.

**How the real thing works [S]:** rivers like this cut off their own bends. The river chips away at the narrow neck until a flood breaks straight through it. The river then flows across the neck, right past the town's land side, and the old bend becomes a still lake. In one night Bend could have docks on a pond and fast river water where its only road comes in. It's a ticking clock that can go off in any flood season you choose.

## The Community's Fear
One of the big powers could march in and take over. Bend can't defend itself against a serious army, so it stays useful to every potential ruler instead: cheaper prices and useful gossip for certain powerful people. Suspects nearby **[S]**: Elturel and its Hellriders downriver, Iriaebor's rulers upriver, merchant houses in Scornubel, and the Zhentarim.

## Places of Worship [S]
| Faith | Why it's in Bend | Annual event |
|---|---|---|
| **Waukeen** (trade, wealth) | The logistics merchants built it. Contracts are witnessed here. | *Ledger Day*: debts forgiven or called in; open-air auction. Keeps the numbers up. |
| **Chauntea** (farming) | The farmers' temple, and the biggest building in town. | Runs the fall harvest festival on **Highharvestide**, a real Realms holiday. |
| **Tymora** (luck) | Travellers, boatmen and gamblers. | *The Coin Regatta*: a barge race around the bend. Keeps the numbers up. |
| **Eldath** (peace, quiet waters) | A small grove shrine on the riverbank. The priestess is half the reason Bend is so safe. | A silent vigil for the river. She's the only one who takes the moving river seriously. |
| **Gond** (craft, invention) | Cranes, winches and mills at the docks. | None. Too busy. Has plans for a canal nobody will fund. |
| **Mask** (thieves, shadows) | No building. Everyone knows the illegal trade has to honour someone. | Unknown, and that's the point. |

Suzy's jewellery commissions could put her in touch with any of these, since temples order holy symbols too.
